## Who is it for
 the salaried person, the freelancer/professional.
## In scope
salary income, the two regimes, deductions, TDS, filing a return, presumptive taxation, advance tax
## Out of scope
company tax, all capital gains, international tax, and anything needing personal advice.
## Law covered: 
Income-tax Act, 2025 and Income-tax Rules, 2026, for Tax Year 2026-27 onwards. Income earned before 1 April 2026 (the 1961 Act) is out of scope.
## Disclaimer
Explains the rules, not tax advice.
