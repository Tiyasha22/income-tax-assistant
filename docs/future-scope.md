

# Future Scope

## V1 scope

V1 focuses on official-document retrieval for Indian salaried employees and domestic freelancers. It answers questions using verified passages from the approved official sources:

- incometaxindia.gov.in
- incometax.gov.in

The system must cite its supporting documents and acknowledge when the available evidence does not answer a question.

## Excluded from V1

- Personal tax calculations and estimates
- Personalized recommendations, including choosing a tax regime
- International income, foreign clients and payments, DTAA, foreign tax credits, and foreign-asset reporting
- Capital gains from shares and mutual funds
- Dividend income
- Detailed tax notices, scrutiny, assessments, and penalties
- Questions that depend on old-law provisions when the relevant old-law content is not part of the approved corpus

## First priority for V2: International freelancer income

International freelance income is a valuable extension because Indian freelancers commonly work with overseas clients. However, answering these questions reliably requires broader coverage of residential status, treaty provisions, foreign tax credits, and foreign-asset reporting.

V1 excludes this area to keep the document collection manageable and reduce the risk of unsupported tax guidance. V2 should add it as a dedicated expansion, with its own source inventory and test questions.
